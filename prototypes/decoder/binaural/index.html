KU100 (SADIE II), 48 kHz. Headphones. The scene is rotated against the head before the decode — what a tracker will drive. Here the mouse drives it.

Sweep turns the head ±60° slowly, hands free. With rotation off the scene turns with the head, as headphones without tracking do.

SOURCES

AMBIX FILE

A 4, 9 or 16 channel AmbiX file (ACN, SN3D), looped. Its order above the decode's is cut.

OUTPUT

LISTEN FOR

Order and method. LS at order 1–3 goes dull and inside the head; MagLS keeps the top end. Order 1 MagLS against order 3 MagLS: how much does the order still buy?

Rotation. Turn toward a source: does it stay put in the world, or drag along? Front–back: a source behind turns to the side when the head turns, which is the cue a static decode never gives.

Elevation. The source above: is it above, or only "somewhere"? Pitch the head up to it.
