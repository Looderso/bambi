The cost: what the dips and the smooth part leave of the measured CLL, blurred to one ERB, over the
      sphere and 4–16 kHz. No support: dips deeper than 3 dB where the measured spectrum is less than half as deep.


    
1 · PREPARE

    
What the fit sees. Blurred along frequency, a dip narrower than the ear resolves is gone; held to an
      order over the sphere, a dip has to agree with its neighbours to be seen. A new setting here fits the head again.

    


    
2 · TRACKS

    
A dip counts where its own spectrum shows it. Dips of neighbouring directions are linked into sheets
      that begin and end; small sheets go.

    


    
3 · SURFACES

    
The largest sheets grow into surfaces, each smooth over the sphere; dips near where a surface passes
      join it, the rest go. Every dip is fitted to the measured CLL — its depth comes from there alone — and each
      surface becomes three patterns of that order over the sphere: centre, depth, width.
