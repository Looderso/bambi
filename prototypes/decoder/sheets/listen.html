Decoder · Nobody's head and the ear slider

The head selected below is the listener: its measurement is the truth. Against it: the KU100, and nobody's head — the population's average of every part, with the ear slider for its notches. Nothing in nobody's head comes from the listener. Does a sound sit where the listener's own head puts it?

THE HEAD SLIDER 4 5

    
A wider or a narrower head: the slider scales nobody's ITD, and the ILD below 1.5 kHz goes with it the way
      it does over the heads — above, the ILD does not follow a head's size and stays the population's. The smooth CLL
      stays too. It leaves 21 µs of the ITD's 34, and 0.88 dB of the low ILD's 1.01.

    


    
THE EAR SLIDER 5 6

    
One template of three notches, the same for every head, learned from half of the people. The slider
      shifts all three along frequency: up for a smaller ear, down for a larger one. Find the place where the sound sits
      as it does measured — then see what the fit against the listener's spectra says (it was fitted on the
      listener's own smooth part, version 6). Depth and sharpness act on versions 5 and 6 too: the template's notches
      are about half as deep and twice as wide as measured ones.
