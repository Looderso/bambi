# 0260 · Any track or group is taken into the field on its own; a group holding something taken is a bus of just that

**Status:** accepted

*2026-10-21. Taking one track of a group took all its siblings, and only two hidden steps gave them back.*

A track or a group is taken into the field on its own, at any depth, and what is not taken plays on in Live where it
is. A group taken is encoded whole, with all it holds. A group not taken that holds something taken is a bus of the
field holding just that, while the rest of it plays through the same group in Live: one group, in two halves.

It replaces "a group is taken whole or split, never both with its tracks", under which a split group put every child
into the field. That made the page modal: an Encoder on one track took its siblings, and the way back led through the
group. Taking per node is what the routing page shows, and nests without a special case.

What it costs: the group's fader, mute and sends reach both halves (the bus plays at the group's fader), but its Live
effects reach only the half still in Live. The field cannot hand an encoded signal back to Live, so there is no way
round that short of encoding the group whole, which remains one click.

A shape an older set kept, with split groups, is read as the tracks it held taken, so the set is heard as it was
saved. A stray mark below the top that the old rule ignored now counts; before release that is accepted.

Overturned if the two halves of a group confuse more than the old modal step did, in use.
