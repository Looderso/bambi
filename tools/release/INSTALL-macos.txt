bambi for macOS: VST3 and CLAP, Apple silicon and Intel

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders:
     VST3/*.vst3  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     CLAP/*.clap  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
   (~/Library is hidden in Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., then type the path.)

2. These alpha builds are not signed. macOS blocks unsigned plugins that came from the internet, so remove
   the quarantine flag once, in Terminal:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/bambi*.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/bambi*.clap

3. Restart your host and rescan its plugins.
