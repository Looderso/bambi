bambi for Linux: VST3 and CLAP, x86-64

1. Copy the plugins into your plugin folders:
     VST3/*.vst3  ->  ~/.vst3/
     CLAP/*.clap  ->  ~/.clap/

2. Restart your host and rescan its plugins.
